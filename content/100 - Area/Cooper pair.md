### Cooper pair
Cooper pair consists of two electrons with opposite spins, and can be regarded as a boson that follows the Bose-Einstein distribution. Bosons can occupy the same quantum state. Therefore, Cooper pairs can simultaneously occupy the lowest energy state which causes the metal become to superconductor at very low temperatures.
