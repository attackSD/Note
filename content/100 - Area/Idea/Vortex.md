## Vortex
&nbsp; Vortex is a small current induced by the magnetic field in superconductor. In the middle of the vortex, the superconductor exchange to normal state.

## Influence
&nbsp; Vortex affects the decoherence of the single junction qubit by applying the current to the junction. The fluctuations of supercurrent in the junction resulting in the pure dephasing of the qubit. Also, the vortex coupled with qubit yield a channel for qubit to loss energy.
