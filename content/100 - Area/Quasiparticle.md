### Quasiparticle
The quasiparticle is a concept used to describe the phenomenon that non-exist particles traveling in a conductor or semi-conductor. In low temperature, quasiparticles usually come from the break of [[Cooper pair]] and quasiparticle usually means ==**electron**==.
