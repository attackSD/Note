## Introduction
&nbsp; Fast and high fidelity readout is important for quantum error correction and quantum computation algorithm. There are outstanding fast readout pulses reach $\sim50 \text{ns}$ integration time with fidelity $F\gt 99\%$. The well-designed circuit parameters provides a highly favorable setting for measurement like $\kappa = 2\chi$ and large effective $\kappa$.
&ebsp; In the conventional readout pulses such as square and gaussian pulse, the waiting time for cavity to ring-down the photon is usually longer in the fast readout setup such as $50\text{ns}$ integration time with $10\kappa^{-1}\approx 200\text{ns}$. This indicate the algorithm acquired more then $200\text{ns}$ to next operation which is not considered in the fast readout research. Thus, we defined an "operation window" which is the time between pulse start and photon relax to zero, this allowed any operation after this window. In the such condition of the same operation window, the comparison between pulse shapes being necessary to obtained the optimal readout pulse shape instead of using a normal square pulse.
