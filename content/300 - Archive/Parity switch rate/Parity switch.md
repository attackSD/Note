### Parity switch
Parity is a property of qubit that resulting from [[Quasiparticle|quasiparticle(QP)]] tunneling. Similar to the ground state and excited state, parity also has two states: odd and even. The difference between of these states is the number of QPs in the island of Josephson junction is odd or even. The parity changes when the breaking of Cooper pairs produce QPs. In other words, the QP induced the parity changes.

### The generation of QPs
The QPs resulting from the breaking of the [[Cooper pair|Cooper pairs]] are called "resident" QPs, and it's typically in the range $10^{-9}-10^{-5}$. However, the production of QPs exceeds the expectation in unknown way. The density of QPs is out of equilibrium when the distribution is thermalized. The conjecture points out that QPs might be ==produced by the stray millimeter-wave photons, radioactive materials, or cosmic rays==. 

### Source of parity switch
There are multiple possible channels for parity switch such as photon-assisted parity switch, qubit excited state induced parity switch and the QP tunneling by the thermal fluctuation^[T Connolly et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. **132**, 217001 (2024)].

In superconducting qubits, the parity results in the different energy in Josephson junction which indicates the frequencies separation between two parity so called [[Charge dispersion]].
The charge dispersion imply the sensitivity of qubit with charge noise especially in the low $E_J/E_C$ device.
