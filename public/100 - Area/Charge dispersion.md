### Charge dispersion
In superconducting qubits, the fermionic parity leads to distinct energy levels across the Josephson junction, resulting in a frequency splitting between the two parity states—an effect known as _charge dispersion_. This dispersion reflects the qubit's sensitivity to charge noise, particularly in devices with a low $E_J/E_C$ ratio.
